Frequently asked questions

How to get an uncontested divorce?

We offer the best online divorce service for a fast and easy uncontested divorce. During your free consultation, our divorce paralegal near you will check if you qualify. If you meet the requirements, we’ll prepare all necessary divorce forms, get them signed, and file them with the court.

Do I need to state a reason for the divorce?

Do I need to state a reason for the divorce? In New York, there are two types of divorce: no-fault divorce and fault divorce. A no-fault divorce can be based on irreconcilable differences lasting over six months or living separately for at least one year.

A fault divorce can be filed for reasons such as:

  • Adultery;
  • Cruel treatment (emotional or physical abuse);
  • Addiction to alcohol or drugs;
  • Sexual deviance;
  • Imprisonment.

How long does a not contested divorce take?

The processing time for divorce cases varies by county in New York. On average, an uncontested divorce takes 30 to 90 days, but in some cases, it may take up to six months.

How is property divided in a divorce?

In New York, assets are divided based on the principle of equitable distribution. This means that all marital property will be fairly divided by the court. Marital property includes real estate, vehicles, bank accounts, furniture, and any other assets acquired during the marriage using shared finances.

Can I file for divorce on my own?

Yes, you can file for an uncontested divorce on your own, but we highly recommend working with an experienced paralegal.
